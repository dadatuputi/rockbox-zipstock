ZipStock: a stock-style Rockbox theme for the Sansa Clip Zip
=============================================================

What it does
- Main menu: one big picture per page with a lower-case name, as the stock
  firmware shows it. Up and Down move between pages, Select opens one.
  Pages: playing, library, resume, folders, radio, settings.
- Lists: five rows in a rounded font, with a blue selection bar.
- Now Playing: cover, author, book, part number, chapter title, a
  progress bar, time gone and time left. Volume shows while you change it.
- Library (the Rockbox database) opens on Audiobooks, which has three lists:
    Authors     author > series > the books in order: "1. Philosopher's
                Stone", "2. Chamber of Secrets"...
    Series      series > the books in order
    All Books   every book by name
  Titles are short ("Order of the Phoenix", not "Harry Potter and the Order
  of the Phoenix"), and a second edition shows its tag first: "5. (Full
  Cast) Order of the Phoenix". After it come the usual music menus, with
  the books left out.
- Long lines scroll sooner and faster than Rockbox's default.
- Each book resumes where you left it, however you open it.

Install
1. Plug the Zip into the Mac. Copy the .rockbox folder from this zip onto
   the player's own drive (not the card), merging it with the .rockbox
   folder already there. Finder hides dot folders: press Cmd+Shift+. to
   show them. Nothing in Rockbox's own files is replaced.
2. Eject, then on the player: Settings > Theme Settings > Browse Theme
   Files > zipstock.
3. Settings > General Settings > Manage Settings > Browse .cfg Files >
   zipstock-setup. This sets the main menu, autoresume and bookmarks. It
   replaces simple.cfg; don't load simple.cfg after it, because simple.cfg
   turns icons off and the main menu pictures come from the icons.
4. Tag the books with their series (once, and again when you add books).
   Rockbox's database only knows what is in the files' tags, so
   tag-books.py, in this zip, writes the series, the number in the series
   and a short title into each .m4b, from what Audiobookshelf says (the
   plugin's index on the card). In Terminal, with the card in the Mac:
       pip3 install mutagen
       python3 tag-books.py /Volumes/MUSICSD/AUDIOBOOKS
   That only shows the list it would write; check it, then add --apply:
       python3 tag-books.py /Volumes/MUSICSD/AUDIOBOOKS --apply
   Only the tags change, in place, not the audio. The old tags are saved,
   and --undo puts them back. Books in no series go under "Standalone
   books".
5. Build the database once: Settings > General Settings > Database >
   Initialize Now. It says "Updating in background" and keeps scanning while
   you use the player; with 35 long books give it several minutes. Then turn
   the player off and on. At boot it asks to finish the database: press
   Select, or wait 5 seconds. Any other button says no. After that the
   database updates itself at boot when files change.
6. Open library > Audiobooks.

How books are found
- A file is an audiobook when its path has a folder named AUDIOBOOKS (on the
  card that is /<microSD1>/AUDIOBOOKS) or it ends in .m4b.
- Books are listed by their album tag (the short title after tag-books.py),
  grouped by the grouping tag (the series) and numbered by the disc number
  tag (the number in the series). A book without an album tag is listed by
  its file name.
- A book in several files gets one line per file: "5. Order of the Phoenix",
  "5.2 Order of the Phoenix" in a series, "Book, part 2" elsewhere.
- The Authors and Series lists start with [All tracks] and [Random]. Rockbox
  adds those to every such list and a theme can't remove them.
- Picking a book queues the whole list, so when a book ends the next one
  starts, at the place you left it.

Covers
Rockbox shows embedded JPEG covers and cover.jpg or folder.jpg beside the
file. It cannot show PNG or progressive JPEG covers; those books get a plain
book picture instead.

To undo
Settings > Theme Settings > Browse Theme Files > cabbiev2 brings back the
default look. Delete /.rockbox/tagnavi_user.config to get Rockbox's own
database menus back.

Fonts are Nunito (SIL Open Font License, see fonts/Nunito-OFL.txt),
converted to Rockbox's anti-aliased format.

Tested in the Rockbox simulator for the Clip Zip, built from Rockbox's
source of 2026-10-25: main menu, lists, Audiobooks (Authors, Series, All
Books, two editions of one book, a book in two files), Now Playing (with and
without a cover, volume, pause), autoresume, folders and radio.
